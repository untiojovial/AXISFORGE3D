# Data availability (draft)

## Public datasets

Used under the licences their sources declare. Third-party meshes are not redistributed.

| Dataset | Reference | Licence | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EGAD | Morrison et al. 2020 | BSD-3-Clause | Both confirmations | official archives; SHA-256 in `research/phase7/egad_selection.json` |
| Google Scanned Objects | Downs et al. 2022 | CC BY 4.0 | Development | the dataset's public distribution, at a pinned commit |
| YCB (16 objects) | Calli et al. | CC BY 4.0 (data) | Development library | meshes from `pybullet-object-models` at commit `57cc400`, regenerated by `tools/verify/ycb_pick.sh` |

## Derived data in the repository

In `research/phase5`, `research/phase6`, `research/phase7` and `research/phase7_extension`:

- part lists;
- the frozen EGAD draw (with per-mesh hashes);
- protocols, hypotheses and freeze records;
- development results;
- the results of both confirmations, with per-object outputs;
- frozen models and their native exports;
- benchmark and audit results.

## Reproducibility package (local, not published)

The `axisforge3d-research-reproducibility` package, built by `tools/package/build_package.py`, excludes:

- DINOv3-derived embeddings (DINOv3 License);
- per-object YCB descriptors, geometry and individual data. Aggregated statistics, tables and identifiers
  are kept.

Each excluded file is listed in `MANIFEST_EXCLUDED.json`, with the reason and the regeneration script.
Excluding a file from the package does not remove it from the Git history.

## Kept local, regenerable

The exhaustive candidate catalogues and the per-trial simulation logs are large. They are regenerable with
the commands in `research/phase7/reproducibility.md` and `research/phase7_extension/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.
