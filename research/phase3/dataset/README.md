## Development collision proxy evidence

The independent development study at [`../robot/contact_probe_development.json`](../robot/contact_probe_development.json) records the revision-pinned MuJoCo adaptation's 32 V-HACD convex pieces per object, hashes for each piece and the original MJCF, and sampled visual-to-proxy contact distances where feasible candidates were found. It covers six validation objects: Animal Crossing, Great Dinos Triceratops, OXO Can Opener, Orbit Bottle, Pineapple Maraca and Threshold Teapot. Four, one and one contact measurements were obtained respectively for Great Dinos, Orbit and Pineapple; the remaining three had no feasible candidate measurements. These are local contact probes, not a global guarantee of mesh agreement, preservation of handles, or a validated grasp/lift success rate. Simulation mass 0.1 kg and friction 0.5 are assumptions. The original manifest remains unchanged and does not point to these separate proxies.

A fresh validation-only download to `/tmp/axisforge-repro-check` reproduced all six OBJ/STL files under the pinned hashes. For example, Pineapple OBJ SHA-256 `218cb4fcfb5833f2d7ed8289a5a960d69121e1b58d82c3bf09ffbbd68fb45b56` and STL `371ddd325335933ea586a13bcd8c10bf5a3f7e351506b0d8bf5b106b9b0d3140` matched the original local dataset. All 30 declared textures are currently present, but texture and MJCF hashes are not pinned in the frozen manifest; their integrity has not been independently established.

`research_grasping.phase3.dataset_mesh.inspect_mesh` accepts OBJ, STL, PLY and GLB and requires explicit units (`m`, `cm`, or `mm`). It converts a copy to metres, reports bounds, face degeneracy and watertightness, and flags sizes outside caller-selected limits. A plausible size is not evidence of a correct physical scale. It performs no automatic repair or collision generation. Install the `trimesh` dependency before using it.
