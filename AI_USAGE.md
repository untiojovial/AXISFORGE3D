## Safeguards

- **No hand-typed numbers.** Every number in the manuscript is a macro generated from committed result files.
  The figures are generated by scripts.
- **Freeze before evaluation.** Confirmatory protocols, models and analysis code were frozen with SHA-256 digests
  committed before the test data were opened (Phase VII; the extension's own freeze). These are digests, not
  signatures.
- **Reviews are not independent.** The internal "reviews" and the adversarial audit were written by the same AI
  system. They are self-checks, not independent peer review. `EXTERNAL_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md` is prepared for an
  independent audit, which does not replace human approval.
- **Negative results kept.** No experiment, negative result or historical result was removed. Corrections are
  logged in `research/phase7_extension/FINAL_CORRECTIONS_REGISTER.md`.
